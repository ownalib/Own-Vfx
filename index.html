Before we start

A few things
you might ask.

Do you edit long-form YouTube videos?

Yes. Long-form storytelling, documentary commentary and podcast cuts are part of the work. Share your footage, references and target length so we can discuss the scope.

Can you create Shorts and Instagram Reels?

Yes. Vertical edits can be built from your footage or cut down from longer videos, with pacing, captions and graphics suited to the format.

Can your team shoot at my business?

Yes. Share your location and preferred dates so we can confirm availability and plan an on-location shoot.

Can I book one project or ongoing editing?

Both. We can discuss a single edit, graphic design work or ongoing creative support for your channel, social page or business.

How do pricing and turnaround work?

They depend on the footage, complexity, deliverables and review rounds. We agree on the scope, quote and timeline before work begins.
