Before we begin

Project details

  • One initial logo direction is selected for refinement. One final identity is delivered.
  • Final logo files include SVG, PDF, and transparent PNG. Editable files for included designs are provided.
  • You supply your business name, approved copy, prices and photography, including any menu, treatment or property details.
  • Builder and Complete include one menu, treatment list or property brochure, within the plan’s total page limit. Choose the format that fits your business.
  • Complete includes 3 agreed packaging or branded collateral items, such as a takeaway pack, business card or appointment card. Item types and sizes are agreed before work begins.
  • 50% upfront; 50% before final file delivery.
  • Estimated timeline: Starter 5–7, Builder 10–14, Complete 15–20 business days, depending on feedback.

*Unlimited revisions cover refinements to the selected direction within the original brief, requested within 14 days of the first presentation. New directions or added deliverables are quoted separately.

Printing, paid fonts, stock assets, naming, trademark searches, and website design are not included.
