How we work & what’s extra

You provide footage, photos, your logo and approved business details. We build your content calendar, edit your Reels and create supporting posts. Your plan covers organic social media management for the listed platforms.

Reels and supporting posts are separate deliverables. Each asset includes up to 2 revision rounds. Premium includes priority revisions within this limit.

On-site filming, ad spend, paid campaigns, extra content and daily customer support are quoted separately. Any extra work and its price are agreed with you before it starts.

We schedule content after your approval and deliver downloadable final exports. Editable project files and paid stock assets are not included. Platform management applies only to the accounts listed in your plan.

Profile optimization covers your bio, profile details and calls to action in the first month. You can reach us on Instagram or email for project updates.

Review and testimonial content is used with permission.

Your first 30-day content roadmap sets the direction. Plans renew monthly, with no long-term lock-in and 30 days’ cancellation notice.
